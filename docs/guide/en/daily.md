# Daily use — staff and CardScanner

## When you arrive and leave

1. On an enrolled device for the correct branch, select **your own name**.
2. Enter your personal four-digit PIN.
3. Select **ΕΝΑΡΞΗ (arrival)** and **Επιβεβαίωση (Confirm)** when you arrive. When leaving, choose **ΛΗΞΗ (departure)** and Confirm.
4. Wait for the sending confirmation and protocol number. If sending is pending, the program keeps the card; tell the owner.

For a split shift, stamp at the start **and end of every interval**. At another branch use its device with the same personal PIN. Do not stamp for another person or share your PIN.

## When to use CardScanner

| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| “Ξαναδοκιμάζω” / sending is delayed | Continue on this page. The card is recorded. Do not repeat the same movement elsewhere. |
| “ΔΕΝ στάλθηκε” (not sent) | Select “Τι κάνω;”, follow the screen and tell the owner. See [failed submissions](failures.md). |
| “Η σελίδα δεν μιλά με τον server” (cannot reach server), or the page will not open | Use the ministry's CardScanner and tell the owner. |

**One real movement, one card.** A pending send may have reached ΕΡΓΑΝΗ with its response lost. Do not repeat that arrival or departure in CardScanner. Keep CardScanner installed for when the page cannot capture a card.

Printable staff sheet: [STAFF-SHEET.md](../../STAFF-SHEET.md) (Greek). For earlier days open [My own history](employee-history.md) with your PIN. You can also ask the owner for an [individual record export](records.md).
