# My own history

On an enrolled device choose **your own name**, enter **your personal PIN**, then select **Οι κινήσεις μου (My movements)**. If your PIN is still temporary, choose your own first. A wrong PIN shows no history; the five-attempt, ten-minute lockout protection applies.

## What you see

Only your own cards from this application across all branches: working day, time, arrival/departure and filing outcome. A confirmation includes its protocol number. Other employees are not shown, and nothing is editable here.

This view does not read today's ministry register. It cannot confirm movements made through CardScanner or corrected by the accountant. “Covered” is not a ministry receipt.

## Another period and finishing

The view starts with the current month. Select **Από / Έως (From / To)** and **Εμφάνιση (Show)** for another period or refreshed outcomes. Dates are working days: a departure after midnight can belong to yesterday. Up to 100 movements appear; **Παλαιότερες κινήσεις (Older movements)** opens the next page.

Select **Τέλος (Finish)** when done. The screen clears after one minute without interaction or when the tab is hidden. Reading again or stamping needs the name and PIN again. Protect the shared screen from people looking over your shoulder.

If you no longer stamp through the application or your account is inactive/exempt, ask the owner for an [individual record export](records.md). Staff reference: [STAFF-SHEET.md](../../STAFF-SHEET.md).
