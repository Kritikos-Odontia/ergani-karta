# Work Card — your guide

Work Card runs on your practice's computer. Each employee stamps their own ΕΝΑΡΞΗ (arrival) and ΛΗΞΗ (departure) with their personal PIN. The program attempts to send each card to ΕΡΓΑΝΗ ΙΙ and keeps the response and receipt.

> **Free, without support and without warranty (EUPL-1.2). Each practice remains responsible for its own ΕΡΓΑΝΗ filings.** No Odontia account or subscription is required.

## What it does

- Saves a card locally before sending. Retries within the sending window; an unconfirmed card reaches a person.
- Keeps the protocol number and proof PDF when ΕΡΓΑΝΗ returns them. A pending PDF is shown separately.
- Retains the local record and reads monthly copies from ΕΡΓΑΝΗ for the employer's archive.
- Serves one employer with multiple branches, each with its own restricted login.
- Shows missed-card indications subject to the [schedule limits](requirements.md).
- Gives each employee [only their own history](employee-history.md) after their own PIN.
- Brings problems together on [owner Home](owner-overview.md), with [settings and branch administration](settings.md).
- Prepares an [accountant export](accountant-export.md) and [reminder calendar](reminder-calendar.md), without filing schedules.
- Keeps [complete backups](backups.md), with guided [new-computer recovery](new-computer.md) and checks before sending resumes.

## Where to start

1. Read [requirements and limits](requirements.md).
2. Install on [Windows](windows.md), [Mac](mac.md) or [Linux](linux.md). For technical installations and remote branches, use [Docker](docker.md).
3. Prepare [restricted users, PINs and devices](access.md).
4. Give staff the [daily instructions](daily.md) and keep CardScanner ready.

**The employer does not stamp for an employee.** The first real card is a true arrival on a real shift, stamped by that employee. Do not send cards as a test.
